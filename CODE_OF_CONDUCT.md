# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project welcomes contributions from everyone. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for all, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help: being respectful of differing viewpoints, accepting
constructive feedback gracefully, focusing on what is best for the project, and
showing empathy toward other people.

Things that don't: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private
harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and any
other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately via GitHub's report feature or by
opening a private security advisory. Reports are reviewed and investigated, and
will be handled in the way judged appropriate to the circumstances. The
maintainer is obliged to respect the privacy and safety of anyone who reports.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
